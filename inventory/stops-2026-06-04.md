## Internal note (not for the app)

The tracker also maintains one **internal-only cross-stop bucket** that does
NOT ship in the publish bundle:

- `general` (282 archival assets) — a holding pen for images that don't
  belong to a specific stop yet (atmospheric Barcelona shots, period portraits
  not yet placed in a scene, search results from broad keyword categories).
  Used for CMS organization only. The publish bundle's `stops.json` excludes
  this bucket; its images don't reach the app unless they're also tagged to a
  listening or walking stop.

The bundle's `stops.schema.json` enum (`listening` | `walking`) reflects this —
the bundle never carries `meta`-type entries.
